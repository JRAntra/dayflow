                How do I add music?

                Add your own sounds

                • Record one: Add a Clip → Record a clip.
                • Choose a file: Add a Clip → Choose a file.
                • Drag files from Finder onto Your clips.
                • The Clips folder: Music → DayFlow Clips. Anything you put there shows up in Your clips straight away; rename or delete a file there and its clip changes too.
                • QuickTime Player: File → New Audio Recording, then save the recording into the Clips folder.
                • Voice Memos: drag a memo out to the Finder (or onto Your clips).

                What works

                mp3, wav, m4a, aac or ogg files. Clips play for up to 30 seconds; for a longer song, you choose which 30 seconds to use, and the full song moves to DayFlow Clips → Originals.

                What doesn't

                Songs from the Music app or Apple Music. They're protected, so no other app can play them. You need an actual audio file.

                New repeated event

                Repeats on

                Each event rings once. On iPhone it rings like an alarm, through silent mode and Focus, until you tap Stop or Done.

                Rename clip
